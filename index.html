Typical Adult Doses - Your Content Widened

Amlodipine 5mg - Hypertension

Dose: 5mg OD, may increase to 10mg OD. How: Swallow with water, with/without food same time daily. Counsel: Ankle swelling, flushing. Storage: Room temp, protect from heat/moisture. Original container.

Furosemide 40mg - Heart failure/edema

Dose: 20-40mg initially morning, individualized. Counsel: Increased urine, take morning to avoid nocturia. Monitor K+.

Enalapril 5mg

Dose: 5mg OD initially titrated by clinician. Counsel: Dry cough possible, report lip/tongue swelling immediately.

Losartan 50mg

Dose: 50mg OD, may adjust. Note: Use if ACE cough.

Bisoprolol 5mg

Dose: HF start very low titrate; HTN/angina 5-10mg OD. Warning: Do not stop abruptly.

GTN SL 0.3-0.6mg

Dose: Place under tongue, sit down. Do not swallow. If pain persists after dose per emergency guidance, seek urgent help. Storage: Tightly closed, heat/light sensitive.

Atorvastatin 10-20mg

Dose: 10-20mg OD titrated. Counsel: Heart-healthy diet, avoid tobacco, exercise as advised.

Warfarin - Highly individualized

Dose: By INR. Same time daily, consistent vitamin K intake, regular INR. Check interactions.

Urgent referral: Severe chest pain with sweating/SOB/nausea, one-sided weakness/facial droop/speech difficulty, severe breathing difficulty, major bleeding/black tarry stool on antithrombotics, severe lip/tongue/throat swelling after ACE inhibitor.

Storage, Expiry, Safety - Full Text from Your File

Expiry principle: FDA: expiration is period product retains strength/quality/purity under labeled conditions. Stability data dependent. EXP 2028-06-30 = do not use after that date. Month/year only = follow manufacturer convention, not guess. MFG ≠ EXP. Lot/batch for traceability. Opened/reconstituted = shorter usable period. Never extend expiry because looks normal. Do NOT use expired antibiotics: less effective, sub-potent exposure drives resistance and treatment failure.

Storage: Tablets/capsules - labeled room temp, dry, original packaging, away from children. Do not transfer to unlabelled containers. Oral suspensions - reconstitute exactly to mark, shake before each dose, follow post-reconstitution storage. Injectables - verify dilution/compatibility/route/infusion time. Refrigerated - maintain range, don't freeze unless permitted. Keep away from heat/moisture/light if label states.

Emergency warning signs: Difficulty breathing/swallowing, face/lip/tongue swelling, collapse/fainting, severe blistering/peeling skin, severe persistent diarrhea with blood/dehydration, confusion/severe weakness/worsening infection, severe tendon pain (fluoroquinolones), jaundice/dark urine (liver), QT risk - palpitations.

Interactions to Counsel: Doxycycline & Ciprofloxacin + Ca/Fe/Mg/Al antacids = reduced absorption - separate. Metronidazole + alcohol = unpleasant reaction - avoid during + product-specific period after. Warfarin + many antibiotics = INR change - monitor. Macrolides/Fluoroquinolones = QT prolongation. Rifampicin = strong enzyme inducer - major interactions. Aminoglycosides + other nephro/ototoxic = increased risk.

Stewardship & Counseling Checklist (Your File)

Confirm prescription completeness/legality, verify identity/age/weight, ask allergies/previous serious reactions, check indication plausibility, check dose/frequency/route/duration/formulation, renal/hepatic, interactions/contraindications, expiry/batch/integrity/storage, give clear instructions in simple language, use teach-back: "Just so I know I explained clearly, can you tell me how you will take these and what you do if you miss a dose?", explain common adverse effects + emergency signs, explain storage + leftover disposal, document & communicate with prescriber.

Patient counseling simple language: Why - bacterial infection, How - exact amount/times, Do not share, Food/water per label, Missed dose - do not automatically double, Side effects - mild stomach upset possible but severe rash/breathing difficulty = urgent help, Storage - original container, Expiry - do not use after date/reconstitution period, Follow-up - return if worsening/no improvement/serious symptoms.

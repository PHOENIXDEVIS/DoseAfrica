CARDIOVASCULAR DRUGS

Example: Amlodipine 5mg - Hypertension. Dose: 5mg once daily. Counseling: take same time, watch ankle swelling. Storage: below 30°C. Check expiry NDA.

Furosemide 40mg - Heart failure, edema. Dose: 20-40mg morning. Counseling: may increase urination, take in morning. Monitor potassium.

ANTIBIOTICS

Amoxicillin 500mg - Dose: 500mg 8-hourly 5-7 days. Complete dose. Storage: dry place.

Remember antimicrobial resistance - use only when prescribed.

Paracetamol 500mg - Dose: 1-2 tabs 6-8hrly, max 4g/day. Counseling: avoid alcohol, check other meds containing paracetamol.

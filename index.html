Understanding Anticancer Therapy

Anticancer medicines are drugs used to treat malignant diseases. They may act by damaging DNA, blocking cell division, interfering with hormone signalling, inhibiting specific molecular targets, stimulating the immune system, or delivering toxic substances selectively to cancer cells.

Important clinical safety notice: The doses shown in this educational database are NOT universal prescriptions. Many anticancer medicines require specialist oncology protocols.
